02 · Where I See the Research Overlap

The overlap is more specific than simply “we both study misinformation.”

What interests me most is that several of the laboratory's research directions treat information not as an isolated object, but as something embedded in behaviour, platforms, networks, and designed social environments. Rather than trying to connect myself to every topic in the lab, these are the areas where I see the clearest relationship with my current research.

CONNECTION 01
Social Media & Web-Service Analysis and Design
ソーシャルメディア・WEBサービスの分析と設計

Misinformation inside communication environments

My current work begins from misinformation detection, but increasingly treats the judgement itself as part of a wider communication environment. This makes the laboratory's work on misinformation, social-media systems, diffusion, and information-system design particularly relevant to the empirical setting in which my research question lives.

Relevant Lab Directions
Analysis and countermeasures for online outrage and misinformation
炎上・デマ分析と対策
Analysis of the relationship between information diffusion and network structure

CONNECTION 02
Complex Network Science
複雑ネットワーク科学

Diffusion is not only an outcome — it may also become a warning signal

My proposal plans to investigate whether communication and diffusion activity can contribute indicators of observational instability. This creates a direct methodological connection with the laboratory's work on network structure, network evolution, and large-scale social networks.

Relevant Lab Directions
Identifying the roles of nodes and links in networks
ネットワーク内のノード・リンクの役割発見
Growth models and prediction of social networks

CONNECTION 03
Informational Health
情報的健康の実現

From isolated predictions toward healthier information systems

I am particularly interested in the broader systems perspective behind informational health: information quality is not determined by one item alone, but also by how people encounter, interpret, and move through an information environment. My proposal similarly shifts attention away from a single classifier output toward the continuing adequacy of the wider observation system.

Relevant Lab Directions

The Shared Object

The real overlap is the information system around misinformation.

The laboratory studies how information is consumed, diffused, structured by networks, and shaped by the design of social and information systems. My current question begins from that same environment and asks: when the conditions supporting an existing misinformation judgement change, how can we recognise that the observation system itself may require review?

Existing Strengths

Social Information Systems

Misinformation, social media, information diffusion, network structure, informational health, and the social application of computational systems.

+
What I Hope to Add

A Cybernetics Perspective

Feedback, recursive observation, adaptation, and human agency — asking not only how an information system behaves, but how the systems used to observe it can remain connected to changing conditions.

Why This Feels Complementary

I am not looking for a laboratory that is already doing exactly the project I have written down. I am looking for one that already understands the systems my question depends on — misinformation, social media, information diffusion, networks, and information environments — while giving me room to bring a Cybernetics perspective on observation, feedback, and human-guided adaptation.

03 · And Then... I Looked at the People

Research matters. But so does the place where research happens.

A PhD is several years of one's life. I therefore care not only about whether the research topics match, but also about whether the environment feels like somewhere people are allowed to remain recognisably human.

I want a research environment in which people are taken seriously without necessarily having to take themselves seriously all the time.

And yes, Professor Toriumi's Google Scholar profile photo also made me smile before I finished looking through the publication list. Small details, perhaps — but memorable ones.

For me, fit is not simply about finding somewhere my proposal can be carried out. It is about finding somewhere it can be challenged, revised, and made better.

From my perspective, Toriumi Laboratory offers a particularly interesting combination: methods that can support the work, research directions that genuinely intersect with my own, and an environment in which I can imagine learning from people who approach those systems from different directions.

And Perhaps One Final Revision

That is why I would be genuinely excited to see what my next research revision might become here.
